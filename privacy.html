FARMeasy — Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

FARMeasy ("the app") is provided by ICAR-Indian Institute of Agricultural Biotechnology (IIAB), Ranchi, India. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

1. Information you enter

  • Farm account: farm name, owner name, optional phone number or email, username, species and breeds kept, district, state and country. Your password is never stored — only a salted one-way hash of it.
  • Farm records: the animal, production, reproduction, health, feeding and sale/culling/death records you choose to enter.

2. Where your information is stored

  • On your phone. All accounts and records are kept in a database on your device and work without internet.
  • In your own Google Sheet (when you tap Sync). If you sign in with Google, the app creates a spreadsheet named "FARMeasy — <your username>" in your Google Drive and copies your farm account and records into it. You own this sheet and can view, share or delete it at any time from Google Drive.
  • Shared ICAR-IIAB demonstration account. Records entered under the shared "IIABfarm" account are synced to a Google Sheet managed by ICAR-IIAB.

3. Google account access

The app requests only the Google Drive permission drive.file. This lets the app see and edit only the files the app itself creates (your FARMeasy sheet). It cannot read your other Drive files, email, contacts or anything else in your Google account. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Other services the app contacts

  • Weather: your district and state are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch the local forecast. No name or account details are sent.
  • AI assistant (optional): when you ask the assistant a question, the question and relevant records from your farm are sent to the AI provider configured in the app (OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic) to generate an answer. Do not use the assistant if you do not want this.

5. Research use

At registration the app asks for your consent for ICAR-IIAB to use farm data for research. Data kept only on your phone or in your own Google Sheet is not collected by ICAR-IIAB unless you share it.

6. We do not

  • sell your information;
  • show advertising or use advertising trackers;
  • access your data in Google Drive other than the sheet the app created.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on your phone. To delete synced data, delete the "FARMeasy — <username>" sheet from your Google Drive. You can remove the app's Google access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

The app is intended for farmers and farm workers and is not directed at children under 13.
