FARMeasy

Livestock farm record-keeping — by ICAR-IIAB, Ranchi

FARMeasy is a free Android app from ICAR-Indian Institute of Agricultural Biotechnology (IIAB), Ranchi, that helps small livestock farmers keep records of their animals — even without internet.

What you can record

  • Pedigree — animal ID, species, breed, sex, date of birth, parents
  • Production — milk, eggs and other products
  • Reproduction — mating and pregnancy diagnosis
  • Health — symptoms, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up reminders
  • Feeding — concentrate, roughage and mineral mixture
  • Sale, culling and death of animals

Built-in tools

  • Feed formulation, breeding value and farm economics calculators
  • Weather alerts for your district
  • Optional AI assistant that answers questions about your own records

Your data

Records are stored on your phone. If you choose to sync, they are copied to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive, so you can open and back up your data yourself. See the privacy policy.
